Simple Code of Conduct

Everyone has the right to freedom of speech and belief, and that right will always be respected here.

If you notice disrespectful behavior—whether directed at you or someone else—please report it to pmh_only@pmh.codes. Each case will be reviewed individually, and appropriate action will be taken, which may include a temporary or permanent ban.

If you believe a decision was made in error and would like to appeal, you're welcome to reach out to the same email address, pmh_only@pmh.codes.
